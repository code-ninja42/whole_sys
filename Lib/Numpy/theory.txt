Why numpy array instead of python list?
numpy array are faster,consume less memory,support vectorized operations 
(vectorized operations means performing operations on an entire array at once without writing loops), provide built-in mathematical functions

np.linspace and np.logspace

np.eye and np.identity

np.rand and np.randn ,np.random.randint, np.choice,np.seed

ravel and flatten

np.resize and np.reshape

stacking and concatenation

split and array_split

copy and view
